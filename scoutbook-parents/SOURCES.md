# Scoutbook Plus for Parents — Source Reference

Compiled 2026-10-10. Companion to the Den Leader deck (`../scoutbook-den-leader/SOURCES.md`). Facts come from Scouting America's Scoutbook Knowledge Base and the official Cub Scout adventure pages on scouting.org. Screenshots come from the same pages.

## Logging In & Your Scout's Profile

- **Scoutbook Plus Tutorial for Parents** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/scoutbook-tutorial-for-parents/
  - Parents should already be connected to their Scouts. Log in at advancements.scouting.org with the my.Scouting account used to register the Scout.
  - Click your child's name to open the Scout's page (rank, advancements, awards). If the child isn't there, contact the local council Registrar.
  - Parents have full control and can edit the Scout's profile (verify info, upload a photo, add a nickname and contact info).
  - Do **not** change First/Middle/Last name or Member ID; they must match the official application. Profile changes flow into official Scouting America records.
- **Why Do Parents Have Full Control?** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/why-do-parents-have-full-control/
  - Parents' full control is not the same as leaders' full control. Parents who are not leaders cannot approve or award advancement.
  - "Completed" means the Scout is ready to be tested; "Approved" is the equivalent of the leader initialing the handbook.
- **Preventing a Parent from Approving Advancements** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/preventing-a-parent-from-approving-advancements/
  - Parents, as only parents, do not have rights to mark any advancements as approved.

## Following Progress

- **Introduction to Scoutbook Plus for Cub Scout Packs** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/introduction-to-scoutbook-plus-for-cub-scout-packs/ (sections "Entering Advancements," "What the Different Advancement Statuses Mean")
  - View More on a rank shows the required adventures at the top and electives at the bottom, with started/completed status.
  - Completed can be entered by a leader, parent, or Scout, on the website or in the Scouting mobile app. Approved = leader sign-off; Awarded is optional.
  - Leaders can back-fill previous ranks; parents and Scouts cannot.

## Marking Requirements Completed

- **Scoutbook Plus Tutorial for Parents**, section "Viewing Advancements and Marking Requirements Complete"
  - "BEFORE MARKING ANYTHING AS COMPLETE, DISCUSS WITH YOUR UNIT LEADERS TO MAKE SURE YOU ARE ALIGNED WITH YOUR UNIT'S ADVANCEMENT PRACTICES."
  - The den leader records requirements completed; parents record family requirements or those assigned as homework.
- **Introduction to Scoutbook Plus for Cub Scout Packs**, sections "Entering Advancements" and "Individual Quick Entry"
  - Click an adventure to mark individual requirements completed (approved is leaders only), add comments, and Save.
  - Quick Entry marks several requirements at once. Parents who are not also leaders, and Scouts, can mark Completed but cannot approve.
  - The resource page line on an adventure links to that adventure's resources.

## Exploring Adventures (scouting.org)

- **Cub Scout Adventures** — https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/adventures/
  - Rank cards for Lion (K), Tiger (1st), Wolf (2nd), Bear (3rd), Webelos (4th), Arrow of Light (5th).
- **Bear Adventures** — https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/adventures/bear/
  - Bear rank: six required adventures plus any two electives. Lists required, elective, and special elective adventures.
  - Special electives (Archery, Slingshot, BB Guns) can only be completed at approved events with qualified instructors.
- **Bear Habitat** — https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/bear-habitat/
  - Adventure pages include a Snapshot, Safety Moment, numbered requirements, and activities ("Choose one activity for each requirement"), each with See Details.

## Calendar & RSVP

- **Scoutbook Plus Tutorial for Parents**, sections "Viewing Troop Information," "RSVPing to an Event as a Parent," "Linking the Calendar With a Third Party Calendar"
  - Select the unit in the upper right and click Calendar in the left menu.
  - RSVP: when set as a parent, click the event; expand My Youth Connection on the right and select your Scout.
  - The link next to each calendar name at the bottom of the calendar page gives an iCal URL (separate for the pack and each den). Google Calendar may take up to 24 hours to show changes.
- **Using Scoutbook Plus Calendar** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/using-internet-advancement-calendar-ia/
  - RSVP is enabled per event with the "Event with RSVP" checkbox. Whether to use Scoutbook RSVP at all is up to each pack or den (presenter guidance).
  - Non-responders show as "Maybe."

## Scout Accounts & the Scouting App

- **Scoutbook Plus Tutorial for Parents**, section "How to Make a my.Scouting.org Account for You or Your Scout," and the notice at the top of the page
  - Scouts with their own my.Scouting account can review advancement and view the unit calendar. You need the Scout's Member ID from the unit leader.
  - Do not share accounts; every my.Scouting user needs a unique account. Scouts no longer need to be "invited."
  - Parents and Scouts can use the Scouting App to access advancements and the unit calendar instead of Scoutbook.
- **Scouting Mobile App for Scoutbook** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/scouting-mobile-app-for-scoutbook/
  - Older article (references legacy scoutbook.com); used only for background, not cited in the deck.
