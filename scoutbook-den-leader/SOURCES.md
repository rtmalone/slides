# Scoutbook Plus Den Leader Training — Source Reference

Compiled 2026-08-11. All facts used in the slide deck are sourced from Scouting America's official Scoutbook Knowledge Base and forums. Screenshots referenced below are from the same source pages and can be pulled directly into slides if desired.

## 1. Login & Overview
- **Introduction to Scoutbook Plus for Cub Scout Packs** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/introduction-to-scoutbook-plus-for-cub-scout-packs/
  - Login at advancements.scouting.org using my.Scouting credentials.
  - After login, use the pulldown menu (top right) to select your Pack leadership position.

## 2. Viewing the Pack Roster / Dens
- **Introduction to Scoutbook Plus for Cub Scout Packs** (same page as above), sections "Creating Dens and Assigning Scouts," "Editing and Advancing Already Existing Dens," "Moving Scouts from one Den to Another"
  - Click the "Den" heading on the Roster page to toggle/display existing dens.
  - Scouts must be moved into a den before advancements can be recorded.
  - Den/roster editing functionality is desktop/laptop only (not available on mobile as of this writing).

## 3. Filtering the Roster for Your Den
- Same page, section "Viewing Adults in your Pack" and "Editing and Advancing Already Existing Dens"
  - Use the **"Show: Filtered"** button to the right of the Roster toolbar to filter the roster (e.g., by den, by Adults, by position).
  - Click **Show Results** to apply/save the filter.

## 4. Viewing Den Adventures / Advancement Progress
- Same page, section "Entering Advancements"
  - Click a Scout's name on the Roster screen to see their advancement progress.
  - Click **View More** to drill into requirements for a rank; shows the 6 required adventures plus elective adventures, and whether each is started/completed.
- **Forum thread confirming there is no single "whole den at a glance" progress view** — https://discussions.scouting.org/t/how-to-see-advancement-for-all-scouts-in-a-den/458826 and https://discussions.scouting.org/t/view-progress-percent-completed-for-entire-den/466555
  - Den leaders currently review progress Scout-by-Scout on the roster, or via Reports (Advancements > History > Quick Report).

## 5. Advancement Status Definitions
- **Started, Completed, Approved, Awarded in Scoutbook Plus** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/awarded-or-approved-options-in-internet-advancement-ia/
  - **Started** — in progress.
  - **Completed** — youth (or a parent/leader on their behalf) marked the item done; still needs leader sign-off. Can be entered via Scoutbook.com or the Scouting mobile app.
  - **Approved** — the unit leader has verified/signed off; this is the official "earned" status.
  - **Awarded** (optional) — tracks that an approved item has been physically presented to the Scout. Note: marking "Awarded" removes the item from open Purchase Orders.
  - Parents who are not also registered leaders, and Scouts themselves, can mark items "Completed" but **cannot** mark "Approved."

## 6. Marking Adventures/Requirements Complete or Approved
- **Introduction to Scoutbook Plus for Cub Scout Packs**, sections "Individual Quick Entry" and "Unit Quick Entry"
  - **Individual Quick Entry**: On a Scout's adventure page, click Quick Entry to mark multiple requirements Completed or Approved at once. Selecting "Approved" auto-selects "Completed." Enter a date and click Save.
  - **Unit Quick Entry** (bulk, multi-Scout): From the Roster screen, select Unit Quick Entry > Enter Adventures. Select Rank, Adventure, and Version, then Apply. Select the Scout(s), select requirement(s) on the right, enter a date, mark Approved (leaders only), and Apply. Scouts must be in the correct den for this to work.
  - Leaders can also enter/backfill advancement for previous ranks for record-keeping.

## 7. Finding Items Awaiting Approval
- Same page, section "How to See if an Advancement is Marked Completed But Not Approved"
  - Click the **Pending Item** tab above the Roster toolbar to see everything marked Completed but not yet Approved, then click into the Scout to act on it.

## 8. Creating a Den Calendar / Events
- **Using Scoutbook Plus Calendar** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/using-internet-advancement-calendar-ia/
  - Set the unit's time zone once under unit Settings before first use.
  - Access via Calendar in the left nav at advancements.scouting.org.
  - To create an event: click a date, then the "+" icon (or the general "+" to add and set the date manually).
  - Choose the calendar(s) the event should appear on (an event can be posted to multiple calendars, e.g., Pack and a specific Den).
  - Event Description is required and visible to all; Notes/Agenda/Minutes are admin-only.
  - Optional: require the standard BSA permission slip; add a location/map link; add up to three reminders; add attendees, including external guests.
  - Event creation/attendance-management permissions are limited to specific registered/functional positions (see permissions graphic on the source page).

## 9. Event RSVP System
- **Using Scoutbook Plus Calendar**, section "Event RSVP"
  - Check the **"Event with RSVP"** box when creating/editing the event so it collects responses (Yes/No; non-responders show as "Maybe").
  - Attendees must be added to the event for RSVP to function.
  - Leaders can enter responses on a member's behalf via **Quick Entry RSVP**.
  - Members RSVP themselves from the Calendar by opening the event and using the RSVP dropdown/button.
  - An **RSVP Report** is available on any RSVP-enabled event.
- **Scouts RSVP to Events** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/scouts-rsvp-to-events/ (returns 404 as of 2026-10-10; removed from the deck's Resources slide)
  - A Scout with their own login can RSVP for themselves and their parents.

## 10. Attendance (post-event)
- **Using Scoutbook Plus Calendar**, section "Attendance"
  - After the event, invitees convert to "attended" status, which leaders can edit (similar to editing invitees).

## 11. Emailing Your Den (Messaging)
- **Introduction to Scoutbook Plus for Cub Scout Packs**, section "Messaging"
  - Pack leaders send messages via the **Messaging** menu item on the left.
- **Forum: "How do I e-mail a crew"** — https://discussions.scouting.org/t/how-do-i-e-mail-a-crew/181787
  - You can click into a specific Den or Patrol to message only that group's recipients, or use "Select All" to message everyone.
- **Formatting Messages** — https://help.scoutbook.scouting.org/knowledge-base/formatting-messages/
  - Scoutbook Plus messages are WYSIWYG (no BBCode required, unlike legacy Scoutbook).
- Note: Text messaging is also supported through this feature but requires the recipient's mobile number, carrier, and opt-in checkbox to be set in their profile.
